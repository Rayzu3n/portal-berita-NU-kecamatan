## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the institutional dignity, grassroots warmth, and civic authority of Nahdlatul Ulama at the kecamatan (district) tier. It unifies three core operational touchpoints: the Public Portal, the authenticated Member Portal, and the administrative Pengurus Dashboard. 

The aesthetic is fundamentally **Editorial & Institutional Modernism**—avoiding the detached, synthetic sheen of generic commercial SaaS, while rejecting bureaucratic rigidity. The interface feels like a well-crafted civic journal combined with modern public service utility: calm, dependable, grounded, and deeply tied to the socio-cultural fabric of Indonesian community governance.

### Target Audience & Touchpoints
1. **The Public (Warga & Umum):** Community members seeking trusted religious guidance, district announcements, transparent reporting, and open events across the 6 Badan Otonom (Banom: IPNU, IPPNU, GP Ansor, Banser, Fatayat NU, Muslimat NU).
2. **Registered Members (Kader & Anggota):** Dedicated members requiring access to their Banom-specific internal agendas, digital KTA (Kartu Tanda Anggota) cards, and district track records.
3. **Pengurus & District Administrators:** Institutional custodians operating multi-role content publishing, membership verification pipelines, and cross-Banom governance workflows.

### Visual Principles
- **Editorial Substance over Decorative Fluff:** Typography, structural dividers, and deliberate whitespace carry visual gravitas. Editorial serif headlines evoke published literature, while clean humanist sans-serif ensures legible administrative tooling.
- **Dignified Green Palette:** Driven by a rich, forest-toned institutional emerald (`#1C775A`), balanced by organic soft neutrals and selective informational accents. Green is treated with reverence, never saturated wall-to-wall.
- **Physical Document Metaphor:** Digital membership cards, verification certificates, and news columns reflect physical, archival legitimacy through subtle borders, generous margins, and authentic visual hierarchy.

## Colors

### Strategic Palette Assignment
The color palette anchors the system in the verified institutional identity of NU, ensuring absolute clarity across public, member, and administrative tiers.

- **Primary Brand (`#1C775A`):** The institutional core. Used for primary calls-to-action, navigation brand marks, verified badges, active side-menu items, and high-emphasis focal links.
- **Secondary Green (`#77DD77`):** Represents vitality, active status, and positive community outcomes. Reserved for success badges, completed activity indicators, secondary buttons, and active status pills.
- **Cyan Accent (`#94DFE0`):** An airy, informational counterweight. Used sparingly for category tags, subtle pill filters, public activity markers, and tertiary data badges.
- **Soft Neutral (`#CFD0C3`):** The structural spine. Replaces harsh black lines with organic, stone-tempered borders, card outlines, table grid dividers, and quiet container surfaces.
- **Lime Accent (`#BFFF00`):** Restricted to high-salience micro-signals. Employed exclusively for urgent announcement pulses, real-time unread pips, and live activity dots. It is strictly prohibited from use on large container backgrounds, interactive buttons, or body text.

### Neutral & Tonal Hierarchy
- **Surfaces:**
  - `#FFFFFF`: Default card surfaces, modals, dropdown menus, and content editing canvases.
  - `#FAFAFA`: Warm page canvas background for news editorial articles and member profile views.
  - `#F4F7F5`: Administrative dashboard application canvas and table headers, infusing a faint green institutional tint without inducing eye fatigue.
- **Typography & Text Contrast:**
  - Primary Text (`#1A201C`): Charcoal-green black with an exceptional 11.5:1 contrast ratio against white surfaces for peak editorial reading.
  - Secondary Metadata (`#5B635E`): Slate gray with olive warmth (5.2:1 contrast ratio), meeting WCAG AAA compliance for timestamps, bylines, and table metadata.
  - Muted Text (`#7B847F`): Form placeholders, disabled labels, and breadcrumb dividers.

## Typography

### Type Pairing Rationale
The typographic system pairs the literary authority of **Newsreader** (display and editorial headlines) with the functional, friendly clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** (interface navigation, dense tables, form controls, and body text). 

- **Newsreader (Editorial & Headlines):** Infuses long-form articles, featured story titles, section headers, and the official district motto with academic prestige and authentic Indonesian publishing heritage.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (UI, Metadata & Body):** Engineered with open counters and balanced ascenders, ensuring crisp readability across compact mobile screens, complex administrative data tables, and official digital KTA cards.

### Scaling and Readability Discipline
- **Long-form Reading:** The `body-lg` (18px/28px) scale is dedicated to News Detail articles, keeping line measure between 60–75 characters (`max-w-2xl` to `max-w-3xl`) for zero cognitive fatigue.
- **Administrative Density:** Tables and dense form surfaces utilize `body-sm` (13px/20px) and `label-sm` (12px/16px) with strict vertical centering.
- **Mobile Readability:** Headings systematically step down on viewports smaller than 768px via dedicated mobile typography tokens, ensuring titles never wrap awkwardly across multiple awkward breaks.

## Layout & Spacing

### Strict 8px Rhythm
Every layout property, padding dimension, margin gap, and structural layout offset strictly adheres to the 8px baseline (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `40px`, `48px`, `56px`, `64px`, `80px`, `96px`). Non-standard units (such as 13px, 19px, or 27px) are strictly forbidden to ensure consistent vertical cadence.

### Viewport Breakdown & Reflow Strategy
The system follows a true mobile-first architecture:

1. **Mobile (360px – 767px):**
   - Single-column linear stack.
   - Screen gutter: `16px`.
   - Cards span full width of the container.
   - Minimum interactive touch target: 44px height.
   - Admin tables convert into individual vertically stacked cards.
   - Fixed top bar with drawer navigation menu; administrative actions shift to a fixed bottom action bar or slide-up sheet.

2. **Tablet (768px – 1023px):**
   - 8-column grid with `24px` gutters.
   - Two-column news and event matrices.
   - Member profile switches into side-by-side personal and organizational cards.
   - Admin sidebar collapses into an icon-rail (72px width) or off-canvas drawer.

3. **Desktop (1024px – 1440px+):**
   - 12-column grid with `32px` gutters.
   - Editorial homepage employs asymmetrical compositions (e.g., 8-column hero story paired with a 4-column secondary bulletin stack).
   - Maximum content constraint: `1280px` for Public and Member portals (to preserve ideal reading widths), expanding to `1440px` for the Pengurus Admin Dashboard to house comprehensive data tables.
   - Persistent administrative sidebar (260px width) with grouped categories: *CONTENT*, *MEMBERSHIP*, *ORGANIZATION*.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Architectural Layering
The design system discards heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering, Subtle Borders, and Diffused Ambient Warmth**. This evokes an organized, physical archival presence rather than a floating application.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):**
   - Background canvas (`#FAFAFA` or `#F4F7F5`). No shadow, no border.
2. **Level 1 (Card Baseline):**
   - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
   - Border: 1px solid `#CFD0C3` (or `#E2E4DC` on soft tints).
   - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 119, 90, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(28, 119, 90, 0.02)`.
   - Purpose: Standard news cards, Banom directory tiles, member data widgets, table rows.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Elevated Cards):**
   - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
   - Border: 1px solid `#1C775A` at 20% opacity.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(28, 119, 90, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(28, 119, 90, 0.04)`.
   - Purpose: Active news hover cards, digital member KTA card preview, active filters.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):**
   - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
   - Border: 1px solid `#CFD0C3`.
   - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(26, 32, 28, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(26, 32, 28, 0.04)`.
   - Backdrop: `rgba(26, 32, 28, 0.6)` with a 4px blur.
   - Purpose: Admin confirmation dialogs (e.g., delete news, reject registration), mobile navigation drawer.

## Shapes

### Roundedness Discipline: Level 2 (Rounded)
The system adopts an intentional, polished **Level 2 (Rounded)** geometric language. This balances community warmth with the structured sobriety of an official institution.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard buttons, text inputs, dropdown menus, table containers, search fields, and small badges.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px - `rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Primary content cards, Banom overview panels, news featured cards, modal dialogue frames, and member ID card containers.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Status indicator tags, category pills, filter chips, and circular icon badges.
- **Avatar & Placeholders:**
  - Square with 12px radius (`rounded-lg`) for official Banom emblems and organization profile logos.
  - Full circle (`rounded-full`) for individual member profiles and administrator avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:**
  - Background: `#1C775A`; Text: `#FFFFFF` (Font: `label-md`).
  - Height: 44px (touch-compliant); Padding: `0 24px`; Border-radius: 8px.
  - Hover: `#165F48`; Active: `#124C3A`; Focus: 2px ring `#1C775A` with 2px offset.
  - Disabled: `#CFD0C3` background, `#7B847F` text, zero elevation.
- **Secondary Button:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`; Text: `#1C775A`; Border: 1.5px solid `#1C775A`.
  - Hover: `#E8F4F0` background; Active: `#D4ECE3`.
- **Subtle / Ghost Button:**
  - Background: Transparent; Text: `#1A201C`.
  - Hover: `#F4F7F5` background; Text: `#1C775A`.
- **Destructive Button (Admin):**
  - Background: `#C53030`; Text: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Hover: `#9B2C2C`. Used exclusively in confirmation modals (e.g., delete activity/member).

### Chips & Badges
- **Banom Tags (Public & Activity):**
  - Padding: `4px 12px`; Border-radius: 9999px; Font: `label-sm`.
  - IPNU / IPPNU: Soft Cyan background (`#EBF8F8`), Text `#0E6E70`, Border 1px solid `#94DFE0`.
  - GP Ansor / Banser: Forest Tint (`#E8F4F0`), Text `#1C775A`, Border 1px solid `#77DD77`.
  - Fatayat / Muslimat: Olive Neutral (`#F4F7F5`), Text `#1A201C`, Border 1px solid `#CFD0C3`.
- **Status Badges:**
  - *Verified / Published:* Background `#E9F7EF`, Text `#1E7E34`, Border `#77DD77`.
  - *Pending Verification / Draft:* Background `#FEF9E7`, Text `#B7791F`, Border `#F6E05E`.
  - *Private Banom Activity:* Background `#1A201C`, Text `#FFFFFF`, with lock icon.
  - *Live Indicator:* Micro-circle (8px) filled with `#BFFF00` and pulse animation.

### Cards & Containers
- **News Card (Editorial):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`; Border: 1px solid `#CFD0C3`; Radius: 16px.
  - Visual structure: 16:9 neutral image placeholder at top, followed by 24px inner padding (`space-24`).
  - Contains Banom/Category tag, `Newsreader` headline, date metadata in `#5B635E`, and author signature.
- **Banom Directory Card:**
  - Minimalist layout featuring Banom title, 2-line institutional description, latest public activity preview, and a full-width subtle link footer.
- **Member KTA Card (Digital Physical Metaphor):**
  - Background: Deep institutional gradient or crisp `#1C775A` framed container with gold/neutral foil border accents (`#CFD0C3`).
  - Dimensions: Proportional to standard ISO ID-1 cards (85.6mm × 53.98mm ratio).
  - Includes: Official NU branding crest placeholder, verified photo box, Full Member Name, Member ID (`NU-XXXX-XXXX`), Banom designation, validity timestamp, and scannable QR verification code placeholder.

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Field Styling:**
  - Height: 44px; Padding: `0 16px`; Background: `#FFFFFF`; Border: 1px solid `#CFD0C3`; Radius: 8px.
  - Text: `#1A201C`; Placeholder: `#7B847F` (`body-md`).
  - Focus: Border `#1C775A` with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(28, 119, 90, 0.15)`.
  - Form groups: Label in `label-sm` with 8px bottom margin (`space-8`). Helper text in `caption` with `#5B635E`.

### Tables & Data Lists (Admin & Records)
- **Table Structure:**
  - Header: `#F4F7F5` background, text in `label-sm` (`#5B635E`), uppercase, 12px vertical padding (`space-16`), bordered with `#CFD0C3`.
  - Row: `#FFFFFF` background; 16px vertical padding; 1px bottom border `#E2E4DC`.
  - Hover: `#FAFAFA`.
  - Responsive Reflow: On mobile (<768px), table rows morph into multi-line card summaries displaying Member Photo, Name, Status Badge, and Action dropdown.

### Dialogs & Empty States
- **Confirmation Dialogs:**
  - Centered modal container (`max-w-md`), 24px padding (`space-24`), rounded-xl (16px). Explicit destructive messaging in `#1A201C`, explicit consequence breakdown, and paired `Cancel` (subtle) / `Confirm` (danger or primary) actions.
- **Empty States:**
  - Centered vertical orientation with 48px padding (`space-48`). Soft neutral geometric icon placeholder in `#CFD0C3`, `headline-sm` title, supporting explanation in `body-md` (`#5B635E`), and primary creation CTA button.
